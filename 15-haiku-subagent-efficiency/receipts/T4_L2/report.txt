It lowercases, collapses each run of non-`[a-z0-9]` characters to one hyphen, strips edge hyphens, and cuts long slugs at the last hyphen that fits in 60 characters, falling back to a hard cut. An empty result returns `'untitled'`.

One edge case the spec does not address: `str.lower()` maps the Kelvin sign (U+212A) to ASCII `k`, so that character survives as a letter rather than becoming a hyphen. I left it as is.
